# Wonder — First cross-resolution audit of the 54 cards

**22 November 2026 · Working research instrument, not a canonical amendment**

## What was actually examined

All 54 entries in [Wonder's open-card source](../fiche_ceathar_54_open_cards.md), against the 15 definitions and dimensional-conjunction rules in [THE_54_CONTROL](https://github.com/TitanicParker/fiche-ceathar/blob/main/THE_54_CONTROL.md). This audit does **not** claim to have independently resolved 54 unfamiliar phenomena. It checks what the cards permit, and where a purported independent X–Y resolution would be difficult.

The cards contain three distinct sorts of evidence: (a) a *coordinate* whose three dimensions mutually constrain one completion-event, (b) an *original X–Y traversal* articulating a movement or relation, and (c) a *compressed utterance* that may foreground a different aspect. None is an exact centre. The source explicitly warns against equating the traversal with the coordinate.

### Non-negotiable constraint

The controlling reference rejects a semantic derivation of the form Transformation → Topology → Persistence. Its dimensions do not operate on one another in sequence. Consequently, **“somewhat X–Y linearly” must not be mistaken for an ordered computation of the three axes**. The X–Y traversal may be followed as an expression, but any triaxial derivation must still be simultaneous dimensional conjunction.

## Nine deliberately difficult cards

These are not “errors.” Each is a test of whether one can recover the same achievement from two formulations **without importing the answer from the known cell number**.

## Two detailed checks

### 05: The absent Y

**Given:** Differentiation × Distributed × Bearing; “X indicates absent Y”; “This here.”

A hasty linear reading: X points to an absent Y, therefore the achievement is about absence and therefore Release. That is invalid. The controlling definition of Release is *de-governance*, not absence. Another hasty reading makes X the focal object and ignores the field that renders its indication intelligible. The candidate reading worth testing is that the presence of X is determinately distinguishable **by its constitutive bearing on an unavailable Y within a context**. The indication is neither a free-standing mark nor a removal. This is **provisional**, because the short traversal does not itself supply the contextual field in enough detail to force the full coordinate.

**Discriminating neighbour:** 01 (Focal mark) would require a privileged identification sufficient in itself; 07 (Opposed standing) requires Release and Standing, not an ongoing contextual bearing. The evidence needed is a scene where the same mark can be identified without indicating an absent Y, and then can indicate Y only through a shared context.

### 27: Local interactions, global result

**Given:** Relating × Release × Efficacy; “X interacts locally to produce global Y”; “Something appears between them.”

A hasty linear reading: there are many local interactions, hence Distributed. But a topology is not a count of participating entities. The candidate coordinate-generated achievement is **a relational efficacy whose completion consists in a previously governing separation or independence losing its authority**. Global Y is not merely a bigger effect; it is the operative coordination that could not be obtained by treating the interacting terms as separately sufficient.

**Discriminating neighbour:** 24 (Relational cycle) is Relating × Distributed × Efficacy: a constitutive operative loop across a field. Both can contain many interactions and consequences. To distinguish them, one needs evidence about *what made the relation complete*: a distributed recurrence or the loss of a prior governor. The X–Y traversal alone does not name that governor, so it must not be invented.

## What the complete 54-card reading supports

1. **The triaxial space is exhaustive by construction over its chosen primitives**: six transformations, three topologies, three persistence modes. That mathematical enumeration is not proof that every phenomenon has a unique or exhaustive empirical assignment.
2. **The X–Y traversals are not uniformly transparent encodings of all three axes.** Several foreground an effect (14, 27), a relation (21), an absence (05), or a transformation (31) that could mislead a resolver who relies on lexical cues. This is an observation about the supplied wording, not a claim that the traversals are defective.
3. **The compression is a third, not always literal, entrance.** Cell 05's “This here” is not a paraphrase of “X indicates absent Y”; Cell 52's “Still itself” does not spell out its Release condition. The omissions may be productive but cannot be treated as evidence of independent resolution.
4. **No independent agreement rate has been established.** Displaying the triaxial answer beside the traversal prevents a blind comparison. A legitimate test must hide the answer, freeze each resolution, then compare.

## Next empirical test: frozen, independent, reversible

**Track A — Coordinate derivation.** Present only the 15 primitive definitions and a coordinate to one resolver. Require an account of the achieved form, a plausible instance, and nearest exclusions; freeze its output *before* revealing canonical names or X–Y expressions. This is consistent with the canonical GPT-D blind-test discipline.

**Track B — Traversal interpretation.** Present an X–Y traversal, with its original syntax but no cell number, coordinate, short utterance or neighbouring cards. Require the resolver to articulate the unresolved question, the achieved change, sufficiency and persistence; freeze its output before revealing the triaxial answer. **This is a proposed experimental extension**, not a protocol claimed to be in the control document.

**Comparison.** Examine whether both tracks independently recover the same *operative achievement*, not merely whether they output the same name. Score concordance, ambiguity, rival coordinates, and what each route failed to articulate. Repeat on new scenes without supplying either canonical representation. Keep failed and ambiguous trials.

**Current result:** An audit and a falsifiable research design; **not** a successful cross-resolution demonstration.
